Material Concept

Not ceramic. Not resin. Not conventional concrete.

VELITH is developed as an architectural mineral alternative for premium washbasin design. It combines the tactile honesty of mineral surfaces with the technical control of a high-performance cementitious casting matrix.

The result is a material language that feels calm, solid, and contemporary — designed for spaces where the washbasin is not simply a fixture, but part of the architectural atmosphere.

White BCSA-based mineral matrix
RUFA reactive ultra-fine mineral technology
Hybrid fiber reinforcement
Low-shrinkage cementitious system concept
Material Technology

Three elements behind the VELITH mineral platform.

A focused mineral casting system developed to support refined surfaces, controlled production, and thin-wall architectural forms.

White BCSA Mineral Matrix

A white BCSA-based mineral casting matrix formulated without polymer or resin, providing the mineral base for VELITH’s matte architectural character.

RUFA Technology

Reactive ultra-fine mineral technology engineered to support flow, particle packing, matrix density, and controlled casting performance.

Hybrid Fiber Reinforcement

Macro- and micro-fiber reinforcement developed to support thin-wall forms, handling strength, crack control, and dimensional stability.

Production approach: VELITH uses vacuum de-airing and air curing under intended production conditions. Surface protection is applied as a separate finishing system for stain resistance and service performance.
