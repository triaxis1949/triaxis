Technology Snapshot

White BCSA + RUFA + Hybrid Fiber

VELITH is built on a technical foundation developed for mineral casting performance, surface quality, and design flexibility.

White BCSA Binder

Provides a clean white mineral base, rapid early strength development, and a low-shrinkage cementitious foundation for architectural casting.

RUFA Technology

Enhances the mineral matrix, supports packing density, contributes to surface refinement, and helps create a dense architectural mineral structure.

Hybrid Fiber

Supports thin-wall casting, handling strength, crack mitigation, and dimensional stability.

Vacuum De-Airing

Helps remove entrapped air, reducing visible pinholes and supporting a smoother surface finish.

Surface Protection

Designed to work with silicate-based densifiers and penetrating stain-resistant architectural sealer systems.

Air Curing

Designed for air curing under intended production conditions, without steam curing or autoclave curing.
