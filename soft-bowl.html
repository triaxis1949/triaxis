Soft Bowl

Architectural Mineral Countertop Washbasin

Smooth. Refined. Timeless. Soft Bowl expresses the quiet side of VELITH through balanced proportions, a slender profile and the tactile character of a soft matte mineral surface.

View SpecificationsBack to Collection
Design

The beauty of material, expressed through simplicity.

Soft Bowl is a low, gently curved round countertop basin designed for calm contemporary interiors. Its smooth exterior allows the mineral surface, integral colour and refined geometry to become the focus rather than decoration.

Material

Architectural mineral character.

Soft Bowl is cast from the VELITH White BCSA-based architectural mineral system, developed with RUFA reactive ultra-fine mineral technology, hybrid fibre reinforcement and vacuum de-airing.

Soft matte surface

Designed to retain the tactile, understated character of a mineral material rather than imitate glossy ceramic.

Thin-wall casting

The material platform supports the restrained profile that defines Soft Bowl.

Vacuum de-airing

Used to reduce entrapped air and support a more refined mineral surface.
