Broad Helix

Architectural Mineral Countertop Washbasin

Sculptural. Textured. Contemporary. Broad Helix brings movement to the VELITH collection through broad diagonal helix texture, controlled shadow and the tactile character of a soft matte mineral surface.

View SpecificationsBack to Collection
Design

Movement resolving into calm.

Broad Helix is a round countertop basin designed around the relationship between movement and restraint. Broad diagonal helix texture animates the lower exterior with light and shadow, while the calmer upper form keeps the basin architectural rather than ornamental.

Material

Architectural mineral character.

Broad Helix is cast from the VELITH White BCSA-based architectural mineral system, developed with RUFA reactive ultra-fine mineral technology, hybrid fibre reinforcement and vacuum de-airing.

Broad helix texture

Diagonal exterior relief creates a controlled play of light and shadow while preserving the basin’s calm architectural character.

Mineral depth

The matte surface and integral mineral character give the sculptural geometry a softer, more tactile presence than a glossy finish.

Vacuum de-airing

Used to reduce entrapped air and support a more refined mineral surface.

Material Technology

White BCSA + RUFA mineral technology.

VELITH uses RUFA (Reactive Ultra-Fine Fly Ash) within its architectural mineral casting platform. Published cement-material research involving Triaxis-supplied RUFA provides independent background on the material; VELITH product performance remains based on its own formulation and product-specific evaluation.
